If you do not set the viewport in your HTML, your website will not display properly on mobile devices. The browser will treat the page as if it were designed for a large desktop screen and shrink it to fit a smaller device, making everything appear very small. As a result, text becomes hard to read, users need to zoom in to see content clearly, and the layout may break or look compressed. In addition, users may have to scroll horizontally to view the whole page, which creates a poor user experience. Therefore, setting the viewport is important to make your website responsive and easy to use on all screen sizes.
